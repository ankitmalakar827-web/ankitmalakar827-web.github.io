PR analysis · Individual

Maggi: from recall to revival

Brand
Nestlé India (Maggi)
Project
A written case study on the brand's crisis communication.
Challenge
In June 2015, FSSAI banned Maggi after tests reported excess lead and MSG. Maggi held about 80% of India's instant noodle market, and trust in a family staple collapsed overnight.
Strategy
Nestlé recalled affected stock, had independent labs retest the product, relabelled the packaging and relaunched with small ₹5 and ₹10 packs. Communication stayed open and emotional: press releases, interviews, social updates and leadership speaking directly to the public.
Execution
I researched the timeline from Nestlé, the Times of India, the Economic Times, the Wall Street Journal and a SAGE business case, then structured the response, impact and gaps.
My role
Sole author. I also proposed what I'd add: a microsite with live lab data, nutritionists, doctors and food bloggers as credible voices, employee advocacy, school food-safety campaigns and structured investor communication.
Results
This is analysis, so there are no results of my own. The figures it cites, such as Maggi recovering about 60% of its market share within a year, come from public sources.
Campaign brief · Team of five

Rapido: "Kab Tak Latakte Rahoge?"

Brand
Rapido (bike taxis)
Project
An ad brief and creatives for Rapido, built for Mumbai's local train commuters. October 2025.
Challenge
Many commuters accept crowded trains and hanging off door rails as routine, and don't see a bike taxi as a real alternative.
Strategy
Target working professionals and students aged 20 to 40 who use the locals. Contrast packed train scenes with relaxed Rapido rides under one line: "Kab Tak Latakte Rahoge?" The brief set a goal of getting at least 20% more of these commuters to download and try Rapido.
Execution
An eight-frame TVC storyboard (from the platform stairs to booking a bike) and digital creatives for Instagram and Facebook.
My role
One of five team members.
Results
Not launched. The 20% figure is the brief's target, not a result.

No coverage secured. This is a writing sample.
Feature article · Group project

Mukesh Mills: where silence tells Mumbai's story

Brand
Editorial piece, not tied to a brand
Project
A long-form feature, bylined 14 December 2025.
Challenge
The mill is known mostly for ghost stories. The piece needed to hold the folklore and the real history without endorsing anything unproven.
Strategy
Treat the haunting reputation as a way into the mill's past as part of Mumbai's textile industry, and state plainly that no paranormal activity has been officially confirmed.
Execution
A narrative feature moving from history to film-shoot legends to what experts say about fear in abandoned places.
My role
Byline author, in a group of four.
Results
Not published externally, as far as I can confirm.
