01 / About

Good work starts with a clear story.

PR and marketing professional with experience across media relations, social media, content, influencer outreach and live events.

I’m Ankit, based in Mumbai. My work sits at the point where a brand’s story meets its audience. That can mean shaping a PR narrative, planning social content, building a campaign idea or coordinating the people needed to bring it to life.

I’ve worked on entertainment PR, social media and brand communication, and I use strategic thinking and hands-on execution to move a brief forward.

How I work: understand the brief, find the useful insight, make the plan clear, then execute with care. I can lead strategy and client communication while collaborating with designers, editors, creators and other specialists when a project needs them.

Available for freelance projects and collaborations in Mumbai and across India.
